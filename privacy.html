Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy for Hossein Eskandari (doing business as Meowy) ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we collect, store, use, and share ("process") your personal information when you use the Meowy mobile application (the "App" or "Services"), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Meowy is a mobile app for photographing real stray cats, pinning them on a shared city map, and building a personal collection. Users can log cats they spot, earn experience points and level up, and explore cats logged by others. The App is free to use and supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase to remove ads.

Signing in is optional — you can browse the map without an account. You only need an account to save cats and use certain features.

Questions or concerns? If you have any questions, please contact us at app.meowy@gmail.com. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Services.

1. What information do we collect?

Information you provide to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register, use the App, or contact us. This may include:

  • Account information — your email address and a Google account identifier, provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Username — the display name you choose or edit within the App.
  • Content you create — photos of cats, along with tags (such as color and type), names, and optional descriptions you add. Cats you post publicly, and their details, are visible to other users on the shared map and in the Explore feed.

Sensitive information. We do not collect or process sensitive personal information.

Payment data. If you purchase Meowy+ (a one-time purchase that removes ads), the transaction is processed through your app store's native in-app purchase system. We receive your purchase and entitlement status through our purchases provider, but we do not collect or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Google Play, Apple, and our purchases provider RevenueCat. You can review their privacy notices here: Google, Apple, and RevenueCat.

Information collected automatically through the App

  • Location information. With your permission, we access your device's GPS location at the moment you log a cat, in order to place that cat on the shared map. We only access location while you are actively using this feature. We do not track your location in the background and do not monitor your movement. If you deny location permission, the App still works — the map simply centers on a default location.
  • Camera and photos. With your permission, we access your camera (to photograph cats) and, if you choose, your photo library (to select existing cat photos). Photos of cats you post publicly are uploaded, in a compressed form, to our storage. Photos you keep private (pets, or cats organized from your camera roll) are not uploaded — they remain references to the photo on your own device. We never move, modify, or delete photos in your device's gallery.
  • Device and usage data. We automatically collect device information such as your IP address, device model and manufacturer, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile carrier, and information about how you use the App (such as features accessed and error/diagnostic reports). This is used to operate and secure the App and for our internal analytics.
  • Push notifications. If you enable them, we may send push notifications about your account or App features. You can turn these off in your device settings.

On-device content checks

To keep the shared map and Explore feed safe, photos submitted for public posting are checked on your device before they are uploaded. This check runs entirely on your phone; photos that do not pass are never uploaded to our servers. Private photos are not checked and are never uploaded.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. How do we process your information?

We process your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and enable sign-in.
  • Provide the App's core features — logging cats, the shared map, your collection, Explore, levels, and streaks.
  • Place cats you log on the shared map at the correct location.
  • Process and restore the Meowy+ purchase.
  • Show and measure ads (for users who have not purchased Meowy+).
  • Moderate public content for safety and respond to reports of inappropriate content.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support.
  • Send you administrative information, such as changes to our terms or policies.
  • Identify usage trends so we can improve the App.
  • Protect the Services, including fraud monitoring and prevention.
  • Comply with our legal obligations.
